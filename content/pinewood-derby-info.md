# Pinewood Derby

The Pinewood Derby is one of the most exciting events of the year! Scouts design and build their own wooden race cars, then compete to see whose car is the fastest.

## What is the Pinewood Derby?

The Pinewood Derby has been a Cub Scout tradition since 1953. Each Scout receives an official Pinewood Derby kit containing a wooden block, plastic wheels, and metal axles. With help from a parent or guardian, Scouts shape and decorate their car, then race it on a specially designed track.

## Important Information

### Kit Distribution

Pinewood Derby kits will be distributed at a pack meeting prior to the race. Each registered Scout will receive one official kit.

### Rules

- Cars must use only the official BSA Pinewood Derby kit components
- Maximum weight: 5.0 ounces
- Maximum width: 2-3/4 inches
- Maximum length: 7 inches
- Wheels and axles must be from the official kit (may be polished but not modified)
- No starting devices, propellants, or loose materials allowed

### Race Day

Details about race day will be announced closer to the event. Plan to arrive early for check-in and weigh-in.

## Tips for a Great Car

1. **Start Early** - Don't wait until the last minute!
2. **Design First** - Sketch your car before cutting
3. **Weight Matters** - Use all 5 ounces for maximum speed
4. **Smooth Axles** - Polish axles for less friction
5. **Straight Alignment** - Make sure wheels are aligned
6. **Have Fun** - It's about the experience, not just winning!

## Awards

Awards are given for:
- **Speed** - Fastest cars in each rank division
- **Design** - Most creative, best paint job, and other design categories
