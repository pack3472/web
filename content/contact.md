# Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you're interested in joining Pack 3472, have questions about Cub Scouting, or want to volunteer, please reach out.

## Email

For general inquiries, please email us at:

**cubmaster@pack3472.org**

## Meeting Location

Pack meetings are held at our charter organization's facility. Contact us for specific location details and directions.

## Join Our Pack

Interested in joining? Here's what you need to know:

1. **Eligibility**: Youth in kindergarten through 5th grade
2. **Registration**: Contact us to attend a meeting and learn about registration
3. **Fees**: Annual registration fees apply (financial assistance available)

## Volunteer

Pack 3472 runs on volunteer power! We're always looking for parents and community members who want to make a difference in young lives.

Volunteer opportunities include:
- Den Leader
- Assistant Den Leader
- Committee Member
- Event Helper
- Merit Badge Counselor

Contact us to learn how you can get involved!
