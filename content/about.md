# About Pack 3472

## Our Mission

Pack 3472 is committed to the mission of the Boy Scouts of America: to prepare young people to make ethical and moral choices over their lifetimes by instilling in them the values of the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

## The Cub Scout Promise

> I promise to do my best
> To do my duty to God and my country,
> To help other people, and
> To obey the Law of the Pack.

## The Law of the Pack

> The Cub Scout follows Akela.
> The Cub Scout helps the pack go.
> The pack helps the Cub Scout grow.
> The Cub Scout gives goodwill.

## Pack Leadership

Our pack is led by dedicated volunteer leaders who are committed to providing a safe, fun, and educational experience for all Scouts.

- **Cubmaster** - Pack Leader
- **Committee Chair** - Administrative Lead
- **Den Leaders** - Lead individual dens by rank

## Meeting Information

Pack meetings are held monthly. Den meetings are held weekly or bi-weekly depending on the den.

For specific meeting times and locations, please [contact us](/contact).

## Join Us

We welcome new families! Cub Scouts is open to youth in kindergarten through 5th grade. Contact us to learn more about joining Pack 3472.
