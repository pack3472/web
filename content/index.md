# Welcome to Cub Scout Pack 3472

Pack 3472 is part of the Boy Scouts of America, dedicated to developing young people's character, citizenship, and mental and physical fitness.

## What is Cub Scouting?

Cub Scouting is a year-round program designed for boys and girls in kindergarten through fifth grade (ages 5-10). The program helps develop:

- **Character Development** - Building strong values and ethics
- **Citizenship Training** - Learning to be responsible community members
- **Personal Fitness** - Promoting physical and mental health
